Principal Investigator

William Severa, PhD

Associate Professor · Computer Science, College of AI, Cyber and Computing, UT San Antonio

Dr. Severa works on the algorithms behind machine learning systems: how to train them, what they can provably compute, and what it takes to run them on real hardware. Some of that is spiking networks and neuromorphic computing; much of it is conventional deep learning under the same constraints. He came to the field from pure mathematics, with a PhD from the University of Florida, and spent the following decade at Sandia National Laboratories. His current work spans remote sensing, scientific computing, and neural systems, with medical applications being an area of growing interest.
